Junjie Wu 伍君杰

👋 Hi!

I am a Senior Researcher on Tencent’s Hunyuan Foundation Model Team, working on models such as Hy4 and Hy4 preview. My work focuses on agentic evaluation (agent behavior analysis and next-generation evaluation pipelines) and efficient benchmarking, with the goal of turning evaluation into actionable feedback for agent development while reducing its cost.

I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from HKUST in January 2026, advised by Prof. Dit-Yan Yeung.

Previously, I was a Research Scientist Intern with Meta’s GenAI team and a visiting Ph.D. student at Yale University, working with Prof. Arman Cohan. I have also collaborated closely with Mo Yu and Lemao Liu at Tencent AI Lab and WeChat AI.
